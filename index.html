Questions we get before an order

A webbing strap closes with hardware (a buckle, D-ring, or ladder-lock) and holds a fixed load path. A hook-and-loop strap closes by pressing two woven faces together, so it adjusts anywhere along its length without hardware. We manufacture both.

Yes, within their design range. We weave the hook-and-loop tape to the same tensile spec as our webbing, so it holds cable bundles, panels, and light fixtures reliably. For high-load or safety-critical rigging, a hardware strap is still the safer choice.

Both product lines run from 12 mm cable-tie widths up to heavy 50–100 mm webbing. We stock 60 standard factory color shades (VT 101 through VT 160) for hook-and-loop, and provide in-house dye-to-match for custom OEM briefs. Browse all 60 shades in our online catalog.

1,000 units per SKU for custom cut straps, or 500 meters for roll tape. We size a production run once we know the width, length, and hardware, so send the spec first and we'll confirm.

Yes. Direct silkscreen printing of logos and exact millimeter dimensions (such as 15x150 or 20x300 mm) on strap tips, woven jacquard branding, heat-transfer logos, or custom buckle finishes can be built into an OEM run.

Nylon webbing and hook-and-loop are both machine-washable and reusable across normal use. Keep hook-and-loop away from high-heat drying. Heat degrades the hook fibres faster than the loop side.
