Every OEM programme starts the same way: built to your drawing. Send a sample, a sketch, or a spec sheet (width, length, hardware, colour, branding) and we loom-weave, cut, and stitch to match it.

  1. 1.0

    Spec

    Send your drawing, sample, or a plain description: width, length, hardware, colour, branding.

  2. 2.0

    Sample

    We loom a swatch and stitch a sample run so you can check fit and finish before committing to volume.

  3. 3.0

    Production

    Once approved, we weave and finish the full order in-house. Minimum order quantity: 1,000 units.

  4. 4.0

    Delivery

    Finished straps ship from our Kathwada facility in Ahmedabad, packed to your specification.

Questions we get before an order

A webbing strap closes with hardware (a buckle, D-ring, or ladder-lock) and holds a fixed load path. A hook-and-loop strap closes by pressing two woven faces together, so it adjusts anywhere along its length without hardware. We manufacture both.

Yes, within their design range. We weave the hook-and-loop tape to the same tensile spec as our webbing, so it holds cable bundles, panels, and light fixtures reliably. For high-load or safety-critical rigging, a hardware strap is still the safer choice.

Both product lines run from narrow cable-tie widths up to heavy 50 mm webbing, in stock colours or a custom shade matched to your brief. Tell us the width and colour when you send a spec.

1,000 units per SKU. We size a production run once we know the width, length, and hardware, so send the spec first and we'll confirm.

Yes. Printed text, woven-in branding, or an embossed buckle can be built into an OEM run. Bring your artwork and we'll confirm what's possible at your quantity.

Nylon webbing and hook-and-loop are both machine-washable and reusable across normal use. Keep hook-and-loop away from high-heat drying. Heat degrades the hook fibres faster than the loop side.
