01 // QUALITY RANGE

Three engineered material grades.

Direct from page 5 of our official catalog: We produce Hook & Loop in three precisely controlled nylon ratios to match exact mechanical load, peel resistance, and target cost.

MAXIMUM DURABILITY

WELPRO-GOLD

100% Pure Virgin Nylon

Our highest-specification fastener tape. Unblended continuous-filament polyamide provides supreme tensile holding power, maximum peel & shear resilience, and unmatched cycle life.

  • Cycle Life: 10,000+ open/close cycles
  • Peel Strength: ≥ 2.4 N/cm
  • Shear Strength: ≥ 11.5 N/cm²
  • Thermal Range: -30°C to +140°C
  • Applications: Aviation, defense vests, orthopedic braces, automotive seating
Quote WELPRO-GOLD →
OPTIMAL PERFORMANCE

WELPRO-PLATINUM

50% Nylon / 50% Polyester

The industry workhorse balance. Combines the high tenacity and elasticity of nylon loops with the dimensional stability and cost advantage of engineered polyester.

  • Cycle Life: 5,000+ open/close cycles
  • Peel Strength: ≥ 1.8 N/cm
  • Shear Strength: ≥ 8.5 N/cm²
  • Thermal Range: -20°C to +110°C
  • Applications: Footwear, safety vests, cable organizers, industrial packaging
Quote WELPRO-PLATINUM →
COMMERCIAL VALUE

WELPRO-PREMIUM

40% Nylon / 60% Polyester

High-volume commercial grade engineered for everyday apparel, light closures, stationery, home textiles, and disposable medical packaging where competitive per-meter costing is critical.

  • Cycle Life: 2,500+ open/close cycles
  • Peel Strength: ≥ 1.3 N/cm
  • Shear Strength: ≥ 6.2 N/cm²
  • Thermal Range: -15°C to +90°C
  • Applications: Garments, bags, caps, insect window screens, craft displays

REFERENCE PHOTO 01 · SLOTTED TIES

Slotted Hook & Loop Cable Ties & Cord Organizers

Precision die-cut head slot allows the strap to loop around itself, anchoring permanently to the cord and providing instant cinch-locking.

Key Engineering Details:

  • Loop-Through Slotted Eyelet: Feeds cord through eyelet so tie stays on the cable when unfastened; prevents misplaced ties.
  • Multi-Cord Application: Earphone cables, USB data cords, power cords, studio A/V patching, server racks, and wiring looms.
  • Gentle on Conductors: Unlike plastic zip ties, soft hook & loop cannot pinch or crush sensitive copper or fiber-optic cores.
  • Color Assortments: Solid Black, Navy Blue, Forest Green, Earth Brown, or custom 60-color VT packs for circuit coding.
Standard Widths: 12 mm, 15 mm, 20 mm
Standard Lengths: 130 mm, 150 mm, 200 mm, 250 mm
Packaging: Bulk 100/500 packs or branded retail blister cards
